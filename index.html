Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Helpful starting points before choosing your wallpaper.

How do I choose the right design?

Consider the room’s lighting, furniture, existing colours and whether you want a statement wall or a subtle background. Save a few favourites and discuss them with Saffron Walls.

How do I measure my wall?

Measure the width and height in feet and multiply them for the approximate area. For multiple walls, calculate each wall separately and add the areas together.

Can I ask about a specific design?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp enquiry button on a design card. The message will include its collection and design number.

Will the wallpaper look exactly like the screen?

Screen settings, lighting, material and print scale can affect the appearance. Treat website images as previews and confirm product details before ordering.

Does the calculator provide the final quantity?

No. It estimates wall area only. Final quantities depend on roll dimensions, pattern matching, wastage and wall conditions.

Can I see a design in my own room?

This page has an illustrative sample-room preview. An accurate preview on your own photograph would require a separate image visualisation service.
